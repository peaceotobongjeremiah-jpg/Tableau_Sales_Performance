# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard — Peace

> A visually engaging sales analytics dashboard designed to provide clear insights into **Profit, Sales Performance, Regions, Categories, and Discounts**.

---

## 📝 Project Overview

The **Sales Performance Dashboard** provides an interactive view of business performance across different regions and product categories.

The dashboard was designed to make it easy for management and stakeholders to quickly identify:

- 💰 Most profitable regions
- 🏆 Best-performing product categories
- 📈 Profit trends
- 🗺️ Geographic sales performance
- 🎯 Category contribution to overall performance
- 💸 Discount distribution

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this dashboard are to:

1. Analyze profit performance across different regions.
2. Compare performance between product categories.
3. Identify high-performing and low-performing regions.
4. Visualize geographic sales distribution.
5. Understand the contribution of each product category.
6. Present business insights in a simple and interactive format.

---

## 📌 Dashboard Highlights

### 🌍 Regional Performance

The dashboard compares four major regions:

| Region | Profit |
|--------|-------:|
| West | $108,418.45 |
| East | $91,522.78 |
| South | $46,749.43 |
| Central | $39,706.36 |

**Key Insight:**  
The **West region** recorded the highest profit, while the **Central region** recorded the lowest.

---

### 🛍️ Category Performance

The dashboard analyzes three major categories:

- 🪑 Furniture
- 📎 Office Supplies
- 💻 Technology

| Category | Profit |
|----------|-------:|
| Technology | $836,154.03 |
| Furniture | $741,999.80 |
| Office Supplies | $719,047.03 |

**Key Insight:**  
**Technology** is the strongest-performing category based on the displayed profit figures.

## 📈 Visualizations Used

The dashboard contains several visual elements:

### 1. 📊 Regional Profit Bar Chart
Shows profit comparison across:

- Central
- East
- South
- West

### 2. 🗺️ Geographic Map
Displays the geographical distribution of sales/profit values.

### 3. 🔲 Regional Treemap
Provides a quick visual comparison of regional performance.

### 4. 📈 Category Trend Chart
Shows performance progression across product categories.

### 5. 🥧 Category Distribution Chart
Illustrates the relative contribution of each category.

### 6. 🔵 Category Bubble Chart
Uses bubble size to visually emphasize category performance.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

From the dashboard:

- 🥇 **West** is the highest-performing region.
- 📉 **Central** has the lowest regional profit.
- 💻 **Technology** is the leading product category.
- 🪑 **Furniture** performs strongly but remains below Technology.
- 📎 **Office Supplies** records the lowest profit among the three categories.
- 🌎 The geographic visualization helps identify where business activity is concentrated.
